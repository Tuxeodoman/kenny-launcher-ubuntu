Kenny Launcher 1.0.13 Linux x86_64

Application binaries, Qt libraries and plugins come unchanged from the verified
KennyLauncher_1.0.13_linux.zip built on Ubuntu 22.04 (source commit 9384363).
The launcher executable is named .KennyLauncher.bin; KennyLauncher is the
distribution wrapper. It selects bundled Java and disables the upstream updater.

Qt 6.11.1 is dynamically linked. Replaceable libraries/plugins are in lib/,
plugins/ and qml/. Exact upstream license texts for Qt 6.11.1 are in licenses/Qt.

symlinks and notices are preserved inside java/<version>/.
